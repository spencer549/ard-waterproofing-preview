Why generic fixes do not hold

Water does not stop at the wall. It waits.

Clay-heavy soil, a high water table in low-lying areas, and Northeast storm systems create hydrostatic pressure that standard sealants cannot address long term.

  • Water along the wall-floor joint after sustained rainfall
  • A chalky white line on the block where water sat
  • A sump pump cycling when it has not rained in a week
  • Musty air upstairs that no dehumidifier fixes
  • Grading and gutters feeding runoff toward the foundation
  • Horizontal cracking in block walls after repeated freeze and thaw

What gets installed

Interior, exterior, or sump. The inspection decides.

Conflicting advice usually reflects a company's preferred product, not your home's drainage profile. These three solve different root causes.

Interior French Drains

Intercept water at the footing inside and carry it to a sump, relieving hydrostatic pressure. Typically completed in a single day.

Exterior French Drains

Excavate to the footing, grade properly, and carry runoff away before it reaches the wall. Landscaping is restored after.

Sump Pump Systems

Sized to your pit's actual drainage volume, never a default unit, and always with battery backup.

Frequently asked questions

Waterproofing in New Jersey

Rising water table, or poor surface drainage?
The entry point and timing tell you. Water along the wall-floor joint, especially near the Passaic River flood zones in Wayne and Little Falls, is typically hydrostatic pressure. Water entering higher on the wall, or appearing shortly after rain starts, is more likely surface runoff from grading or overwhelmed gutters. In older homes near Parsippany-Troy Hills both often occur together.
Why do companies disagree on interior versus exterior?
Both are legitimate for different root causes. Interior drains suit hydrostatic pressure from a high water table, common in low-lying Passaic and West Essex County. Exterior excavation suits surface runoff, typical on hillier Morris County terrain. Conflicting advice usually means a company is defaulting to its preferred system rather than diagnosing yours.
What if hydrostatic pressure is left untreated?
Saturated soil freezes, expands against the concrete, thaws, and repeats each season. That movement widens pores in poured concrete and causes horizontal cracking in block walls. Seepage eventually becomes structural movement. Older homes around Morristown built in the 1950s and 1960s are especially susceptible.
How is a sump pump sized for a flood zone?
By the drainage volume the system must manage, which depends on basement square footage, soil absorption around the foundation, and how fast the water table rises in a storm. Homes near the Passaic River need far more capacity than hillside lots in Morris County. Every ARD installation is sized to the property and includes battery backup.
How fast can you reach me, and are there travel fees?
ARD dispatches from West Caldwell and serves all of West Essex, Union, Passaic, and Morris Counties, areas the crews already work in. Standard site visits are typically next day. For an active flood, call or text any time. ARD is open 24 hours.
What hidden costs should I watch for?
Scope changes after work begins are the most common surprise. ARD honors its original quote even when a job turns out more complex. The other risk is inadequate sizing, where a pump too small for your volume needs replacing within a few storm seasons. Ask how the sump was sized and what backup is included before signing.
