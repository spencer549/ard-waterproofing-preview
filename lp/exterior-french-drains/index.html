Common questions

What exactly is an exterior French drain?

An exterior French drain is a waterproofing system designed to intercept and redirect groundwater away from your home's foundation. It includes a sloped trench lined with filter fabric, filled with clean gravel, and contains a perforated drain pipe wrapped in fabric for long-lasting protection against water intrusion and hydrostatic pressure.

What water problems does a French drain solve?

French drains prevent basement and crawl space flooding, reduce hydrostatic pressure on foundation walls, eliminate soggy lawns near your home, and protect the structural integrity of your home by effectively managing subsurface water around your foundation.

How do I know if a French drain is right for my home?

If your home suffers from water pooling, damp basements, or foundation pressure caused by subsurface groundwater, an exterior French drain is likely the best permanent solution. Our free on-site evaluation will determine if this system fits your needs.

Will the installation damage my landscaping?

our crews carefully excavate and install your French drain with minimal disruption. After installation, we backfill and grade the area to restore your lawn's appearance, ensuring a clean, smooth finish.

How long does installation take?

Typical residential French drain installations are completed within a few days, depending on the system size and complexity. Our team works efficiently to restore your home's protection quickly.

What are the risks of DIY French drain installation?

DIY installation often leads to improper slope, clogged pipes, or unstable trenching, which can cause system failure and costly repairs. ARD Waterproofing uses commercial-grade materials and expert techniques to guarantee lasting performance and safety.

Does ARD Waterproofing provide a warranty?

Yes. We offer a comprehensive warranty on all exterior French drain installations, ensuring your investment is protected for the long term and providing you with peace of mind.
