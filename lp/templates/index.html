The four reasons a New Jersey basement takes on water

The fix, the disruption and the price are different for each one. This is why a quote given over the phone without anyone looking at the property is a guess.

1

Hydrostatic pressure under the slab

The water table rises after rain and pushes up. It comes in at the joint where the wall meets the floor. Needs an interior perimeter drain, not sealing.

2

Surface water at the foundation

Grading, gutters and downspouts are delivering water to the wall. Often the cheapest fix on this list, and often misdiagnosed as something worse.

3

Cracks in the foundation wall

A direct route through the wall itself. Repairable, but only worth doing once you know it is not also carrying pressure from below.

4

A pump that cannot keep up

Undersized, failed, or discharging somewhere the water simply returns from. Common, and the one homeowners most often find first.
